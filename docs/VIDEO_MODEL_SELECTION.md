# Video route intent

This reference describes initial route planning for generic manifest pipelines.
New one-shot creator-command factory work uses only the route and native controls
in its current standing setup and exact plan. Its route does not change after
dispatch. See [`production-factory.md`](production-factory.md). Historical projects
retain their saved route and authority records.

## Initial route selection

A creator may name an exact route, prefer a route while allowing initial planning
to choose another eligible route in the same approved pool, or ask the planner to
choose within that pool. Exact intent never substitutes. A same-named model under
different providers must include its provider. Managed-media routes such as Grok
CLI may have no selectable model; never place the agent's language model in a media
model field.

The `video_selector` input uses `model_selection_intent` and an explicit
`approved_pool`. Planning checks the exact model/mode form, controls, account and
transport. It preserves settings and reports unsupported or missing controls rather
than dropping them. Planning or ranking makes no account call and grants no dispatch
authority. Generic pipeline execution retains its manifest's current admission,
review and approval requirements.

Unknown cost, charge or quota remains unknown, never free or zero. An unknown-cost
OpenArt route has no enforceable credit ceiling; attempt caps limit calls, not spend.
Do not purchase plans, credits or top-ups, add credentials or substitute a paid API.
CLI and MCP are distinct transports and accounts; permission for one does not
authorize the other. Never add a provider silently or promise automatic quality
gains.

## Capability and evidence limits

Native-form support establishes whether a request can be expressed, not visual
quality, continuity, dialogue fidelity or comparative superiority. Provider
documentation is not connector observation. An earlier successful route result
applies only to its observed settings, account and transport; it does not establish
current readiness or authorize future work.

The October 7, 2026 qualification record documents one collected reference-free
PixVerse V6 text-to-video result: 1 second, 16:9, 720p, 1280×720 H.264 at 24fps,
with 1.041667 seconds of decoded video. It had one original submission and no
upload. Transport and bytes were verified; creative quality was not certified. This
result does not establish comparative quality, general account entitlement, price
or affordability. See [`implementation/2026-10-07-openart-live-720p-test.md`](implementation/2026-10-07-openart-live-720p-test.md).

OpenArt reference-free cutaways are available only when a generic pipeline permits
a genuinely independent text-only shot. They cannot satisfy cast, dialogue,
reference or frame-pin requirements. Keep current route evidence with the selected
pipeline's records.
